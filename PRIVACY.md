# Privacy Policy

iDive for IBM i (macOS, Windows) and IBMi-iDive Mobile (iPad), by Bob Cozzi.

Effective November 23, 2026.

## Summary

iDive connects directly from your device to the IBM i systems you choose. It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## What the app handles

- **Connection details**: host names, user profile names, and your connection preferences. These are stored only on your device.
- **Passwords**: stored only if you turn on "Save password", in the operating system's secure credential store (the Keychain on Apple devices). Passwords are sent only to the IBM i system you connect to.
- **Your work**: SQL and CL scripts, statement history, query results, and spooled file or IFS downloads you request. These stay on your device and on your own IBM i. The optional Auto Recall feature saves your editor script either to a file on your device or to the iDive folder in your own IBM i home directory, as you choose.
- **Files you export**: results, spooled files, and IFS files you save are written to locations you pick on your device.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- It does not contact any server other than the IBM i systems you connect to.
- It does not share data with third parties.
- It does not use your location. The iPad app's operating system settings include a location usage description only because a system component requires one.

## Security

Connections to IBM i can use TLS when your system is configured for it. You control whether to enable "Secure (TLS)" for each connection. Data you store on your device is protected by your device's own security.

## Your choices

You can delete saved connections, saved passwords, and saved scripts in the app at any time, or remove all app data by deleting the app.

## Children

The app is a professional tool for IBM i administrators and developers and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
